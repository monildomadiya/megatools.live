MegaTools publishes calculators. It does not give advice. This page explains the difference, because on some of these topics the distinction genuinely matters.

## What a calculator here does

It takes the numbers you enter, applies a published formula, and shows you the result along with the formula, a worked example, and the sources it came from.

That is the whole of it. The tool does not know your medical history, your tax residency, your credit file, your other debts, or anything else about your situation. It applies an equation, and equations do not have judgement.

## Health and fitness

Nothing on this site is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation, and using it does not create any clinical relationship.

The health formulas here — BMI, basal metabolic rate, body fat estimates, calorie requirements — were derived from studies of populations. They describe averages. Applied to one person they can be substantially wrong, and each tool's page says where and why.

**Do not use these tools to make a medical decision.** Do not use them to justify delaying care. If a result concerns you, or contradicts something a clinician has told you, the clinician has more information than the calculator does.

Talk to a doctor before starting a diet, a fasting protocol, or a training programme, particularly if you are pregnant, have an eating disorder or a history of one, are managing a chronic condition, or are taking medication that affects weight or metabolism.

## Money, tax, and property

Nothing here is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and we are not licensed to provide any of it.

Financial calculators depend heavily on assumptions the tool has to make on your behalf: how interest compounds, whether rates stay fixed, how fees are treated, which deductions apply. Every page states its assumptions, and a real lender, tax authority, or insurer may work differently.

Two consequences follow. A loan or mortgage figure here is an estimate, not an offer — the number in a lender's binding quote is the one that counts. And a tax or take-home figure is a rough guide only, because real liability depends on allowances, credits, and circumstances no general calculator can capture.

Investment projections are arithmetic run forward on assumptions you supplied. They are not forecasts. Past returns do not predict future ones, and a compound interest curve is not a promise.

Speak to a qualified adviser, accountant, or broker before acting.

## Accuracy and currency

We check every calculator by hand and cite primary sources, but errors are possible and standards move. Tax rates change, health bodies revise guidance, and a page that was current last year may not be today. Each page shows when it was last updated.

If you find something wrong, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact).
