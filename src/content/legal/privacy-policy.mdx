This policy explains what MegaTools collects, what it does not, and what control you have. It covers the website at megatools.live.

## The short version

The numbers you type into a calculator never leave your device. Every calculation on this site runs in your browser, so there is no server that receives your weight, your salary, or your loan balance — there is nothing to receive it.

One tool is not a calculation and does not work this way. The domain age checker looks a domain up in the public registry, which means the domain name you type is sent to a third-party registry service. It is the only tool that reaches the network, it says so on its own page, and it is described in full below.

What we do collect is ordinary website analytics, and advertising on the site is served by Google, which sets its own cookies. Both are described in detail below, and both are things you can turn off.

## What we do not collect

We want to be specific, because "we value your privacy" boilerplate tells you nothing.

**Calculator inputs.** Height, weight, income, loan amounts, waist measurements, text you paste into a counter — all of it is processed by JavaScript running on your own device. None of it is transmitted to us, logged, or stored. You can verify this by opening your browser's developer tools, switching to the Network tab, and using any calculator on the site: no request is made. The domain age checker is the single exception and is covered in its own section below.

## The one tool that makes a request

The domain age checker cannot work the way the calculators do. A domain's registration date is held by the registry that issued it, not by anything on your device, so looking it up means asking the registry.

When you press the lookup button on that page, your browser sends the domain name you entered directly to `rdap.org`, a public bootstrap service for the Registration Data Access Protocol, which forwards the query to the registry responsible for that top-level domain. The request goes from your browser to that service. It does not pass through us, we do not see it, and we do not log or store it — but it does leave your device, and calling that private would be wrong.

Two things follow. Those third parties will see the domain you asked about and your IP address, and their own privacy terms apply to that, not ours. And nothing is sent until you press the button: typing in the field makes no request, so a domain you type and then think better of is never transmitted.

Every other tool on the site behaves exactly as described in the section above.

**Accounts.** There are none. We do not ask for an email address to use a tool, and there is no login.

**Files.** Nothing you paste or type is uploaded.

## What we do collect

### Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages people reach and roughly where visitors come from, so we know which tools to build next. It records things like the page you viewed, the referring site, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and general device and browser information.

We have IP anonymisation enabled, which means Google truncates your IP address before storing it.

Analytics is measured in aggregate. We do not use it to identify individual visitors, and we have not enabled Google Signals or any cross-device identity feature.

### Advertising

Display advertising on this site is served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads and to measure their performance. Depending on your settings and location, those ads may be personalised using data Google has collected about your browsing across other sites.

We do not receive that data. We see aggregate performance reporting — impressions, clicks, revenue — with no information about who you are.

You can control how Google personalises ads for you at [Google Ads Settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/), and you can opt out of personalised advertising from participating vendors at [YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) or [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/) in Europe.

### Server logs

Our web server keeps standard access logs — IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent — for security and troubleshooting. These are retained for 30 days and then deleted.
