A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store. This page lists every cookie that can appear while you use MegaTools, who sets it, what it does, and how long it lasts.

## MegaTools sets no cookies of its own

There is no login, no session, no saved preference, and no shopping basket, so there is nothing for a first-party cookie to hold. Calculator inputs are held in memory while the page is open and are gone when you close the tab.

Every cookie below is set by a third party — Google — for analytics or advertising.

## Analytics cookies

Set by Google Analytics 4 to measure which pages are visited and how people arrive at the site.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes one browser from another so repeat visits are not counted as new visitors | 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Maintains session state for the GA4 property | 2 years |

We have IP anonymisation enabled, so Google truncates your IP address before it is stored, and we have not enabled cross-device tracking features.

## Advertising cookies

Where display advertising is shown, Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to serve ads, cap how often you see the same ad, and measure performance. Depending on your location and settings, these may be used to personalise the ads you see based on your browsing on other sites.

Common examples include Google's `IDE` and `test_cookie` on the doubleclick.net domain, and `__gads` and `__gpi`. The exact set varies by which ad partners participate in a given auction, so this cannot be an exhaustive list — Google publishes its current inventory in its [advertising cookie documentation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies are only set after you agree through the consent banner. Nothing non-essential is set before you make a choice.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the consent link in the site footer, and withdrawing it stops further non-essential cookies from being set.

Outside those regions, cookies may be set on arrival in line with local law, and you can still opt out using the controls below.

## Turning cookies off

**In your browser.** Every major browser can block or delete cookies. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all expose this under privacy or site settings. Blocking all cookies will not break this site — the calculators do not use them.

**For advertising specifically.** Google's ad personalisation controls are at [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). Industry-wide opt-outs are available at [YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) in the US and [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/) in Europe.

**For Google Analytics specifically.** Google publishes a [browser opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) that blocks GA measurement across every site you visit.

## Do Not Track

We do not respond to Do Not Track browser signals, because there is still no agreed standard for what a site should do when it receives one. We do respond to the Global Privacy Control signal where applicable law requires it.
